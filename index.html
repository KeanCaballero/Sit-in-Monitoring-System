About the System

CCS Sit-in Monitoring System — University of Cebu

Our Purpose

The CCS Sit-in Monitoring System is designed to streamline the management of student sit-in sessions in the College of Computer Studies laboratories. It replaces manual logbook tracking with a fast, digital solution that benefits both students and administrators.

Laboratory Coverage

The system covers four CCS computer labs — Lab 524, Lab 526, Lab 528, and Lab 530. Each lab has 40 PCs that students can view and reserve in real time through the student dashboard.

For Students

Students can reserve a PC, monitor their sit-in history, check remaining sessions (30 per semester), earn points for completed sessions, and view the leaderboard. All features are accessible after a one-time registration.

For Administrators

CCS lab administrators can log students in and out, view real-time sit-in activity, manage reservations, generate reports, post announcements, award points, and monitor feedback — all from a single secure admin dashboard.

Technology Stack

Built with PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap 5, and Chart.js. Hosted locally via XAMPP for the University of Cebu CCS department. The system was developed as a final capstone project by BS Computer Science students.

Need Help?
Contact the CCS Lab Administrator or visit the laboratory during operating hours.
