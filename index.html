About the System

CCS Sit-in Monitoring System — University of Cebu

Our Purpose

The CCS Sit-in Monitoring System streamlines management of student sit-in sessions in the College of Computer Studies labs, replacing manual logbook tracking with a fast digital solution.

Laboratory Coverage

Covers four CCS computer labs — Lab 524, Lab 526, Lab 528, and Lab 530. Each lab has 40 PCs viewable and reservable in real time.

For Students

Reserve a PC, monitor sit-in history, check remaining sessions (30 per semester), earn points, and view the leaderboard after a one-time registration.

For Administrators

Log students in/out, view real-time activity, manage reservations, generate reports, post announcements, and award points from a single secure dashboard.

Technology Stack

Built with PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap 5, and Chart.js. Hosted via XAMPP for the UC CCS department. Developed as a capstone project by BS Computer Science students.
